Privacy

What this directory stores

This catalog is a static website. We do not create shopper accounts and we do not process orders.

On this site

Optional product comments (name, email, review text) stay in your browser with localStorage. Other visitors cannot see them. Clearing site data deletes them.

On the agent

When you tap Buy on Kakobuy or Choose buy, you leave this site. Payment, warehouse, and shipping data are handled by that agent under their own terms.
